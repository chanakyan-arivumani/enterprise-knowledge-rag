Employees can work remotely up to three days per week. Remote work schedules require approval from the employee's manager.

The annual learning allowance is INR 30000 per employee. It can be used for approved courses, books, and professional certifications.

Travel expense claims must be submitted within 15 days of returning. Each claim must include itemized receipts and the relevant project code.

Production database access requires approval from the security team. Temporary access expires automatically after eight hours.

Customer data backups run every six hours. Backups are retained for 30 days in a separate storage location.

Lost company laptops must be reported to the IT team within one hour of discovery. The IT team will revoke active sessions and initiate a remote wipe.
